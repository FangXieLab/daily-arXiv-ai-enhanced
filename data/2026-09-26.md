### [1] [Back to the Definition: Estimating Step-Level Advantages via Trajectory Graphs for Agentic Reinforcement Learning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.28963)
*Xincheng Yao,Haobo Fu,Weiming Liu,Chongyang Zhang*

Main category: cs.AI

TL;DR: 本文提出 GRAFT，一个基于图的步级信用分配框架，通过在轨迹图上进行贝尔曼迭代恢复节点状态值并按节点值差分配信用，实现忠实于 RL 基本定义的步级优势估计，并提出 Graph GAE 降低偏差，在多轮智能体基准上优于 GRPO 及近期算法。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 基于组的强化学习（如 GRPO）在响应级优势估计可靠，但在步级存在系统性偏差：轨迹级优势过于粗糙，难以准确反映单步贡献（失败轨迹中也可能包含有价值的步骤）。作者发现 GRPO 的成功源于其优势估计策略符合 RL 基本定义——同一状态多次采样的平均奖励即可信的状态值估计。若要将这种忠实估计扩展到步级，原则上需要对每个中间状态采样多个动作，但逐状态采样成本过高。

Method: 提出 GRAFT（Graph-based Faithful sTep-level credit-assignment）框架：将所有 rollout 轨迹嫁接为一张轨迹图，在图上通过贝尔曼迭代恢复节点状态值，并以节点值之差为每条边（即每个步骤）分配信用。此外提出 Graph GAE，将 GAE 扩展到轨迹图上，以降低状态值估计偏差带来的影响。

Result: 在多轮智能体（agentic）基准上的实验显示，GRAFT 相较 GRPO 取得一致的性能提升，并优于近期提出的智能体 RL 算法。理论上证明所估计的步级优势忠实符合 RL 中优势的基本定义。代码将发布于 https://github.com/xcyao00/GRAFT。

Conclusion: GRAFT 通过将轨迹建模为图并借助贝尔曼迭代实现忠实的步级信用分配，配合 Graph GAE 提升估计可靠性，为多轮智能体 LLM 的强化学习训练提供了一种更精确、有效的步级优势估计范式。

Abstract: Group-based reinforcement learning (RL) methods, such as GRPO and its variants, have become a leading paradigm for training reasoning and agentic large language models (LLMs). While their group-normalized advantage estimation is reliable at the response level, it becomes systematically biased at the step level, since coarse-grained trajectory-level advantages are hard to accurately reflect the contribution of individual steps (i.e, failed trajectories may contain valuable steps). Revisiting the foundational RL definition, we notice that GRPO's success on single-turn tasks stems from its advantage estimation strategy, which adheres to the basic definition: the mean reward of multiple actions sampled from the same state constitutes a credible state-value estimate. Extending the faithful estimation to step-level would in principle demand sampling multiple actions from each intermediate state, which is too costly on a per-state basis. Theoretically, the estimated step-level advantage faithfully adheres to the basic advantage definition in RL. To further ensure the reliability of step-level advantage estimation, we further propose Graph GAE, which extends GAE to the trajectory graph for reducing the impact of state-value estimation bias. Experiments across a range of multi-turn agentic benchmarks show consistent gains over GRPO and superior performance compared to recent agentic RL algorithms. Code will be available at https://github.com/xcyao00/GRAFT.

We observe the same pattern after a real tool-agent parameter update. Overall, our results show that agents do not need to recompute action credit after every policy update: much of the historical evidence can be reused or cheaply corrected, reducing the additional interaction required to keep action decisions up to date.
